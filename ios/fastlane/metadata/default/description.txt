Deddal is an all-in-one resource, designed for speed cubers, for learning and mastering CFOP algorithms with interactive training tools.

Features:
	•	CFOP Case Library – Find F2L, 2-Look OLL, 2-Look PLL, and full OLL/PLL sets.
	•	Algorithm Variations – Explore multiple algorithms per case to find the most efficient and natural solutions.
	•	Algorithm Player – Step-by-step visualization to figure execution flow and enhance memorization.
